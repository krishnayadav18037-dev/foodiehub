01

Terms & Conditions

By creating an account or placing an order on FoodieHub, you agree to use the platform lawfully and in good faith. FoodieHub acts as a marketplace connecting customers with independent restaurant partners and does not itself prepare food.

  • You must be at least 18 years old to place an order.
  • Menu prices and availability are set by individual restaurant partners and may change without notice.
  • FoodieHub reserves the right to suspend accounts found to be in violation of these terms.
02

Privacy Policy

We collect the information needed to process your orders, such as your name, delivery address, contact details and payment preference. This information is used solely to fulfil orders, improve our service and communicate with you about your account.

  • We never sell your personal information to third parties.
  • Delivery details are shared only with the assigned rider and restaurant partner for the purpose of your order.
  • You may request a copy or deletion of your data at any time by contacting support.
03

Refund Policy

If your order arrives incorrect, incomplete, or significantly delayed, you may be eligible for a full or partial refund. Refund requests should be submitted within 24 hours of delivery through the support team.

  • Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
  • Cash on Delivery refunds are issued as account credit unless otherwise requested.
  • Refunds are not guaranteed for change-of-mind cancellations made after preparation has started.
04

Delivery Policy

FoodieHub aims to deliver every order as quickly and safely as possible. Estimated delivery times shown at checkout are approximate and may vary due to weather, traffic or order volume.

  • A small delivery fee applies to most orders and is shown before checkout.
  • Riders will attempt to contact you if they are unable to locate your address.
  • Orders left unattended after multiple delivery attempts may be cancelled without refund.
05

User Responsibilities

To keep FoodieHub safe and reliable for everyone, customers agree to:

  • Provide accurate delivery addresses and contact information.
  • Be reasonably available to receive deliveries at the scheduled time.
  • Treat delivery riders and restaurant staff with respect and courtesy.
  • Report any issues with an order promptly and honestly through official support channels.
