Our story

Good food, honest delivery, real people behind it

FoodieHub started as a simple idea: make it effortless for people to enjoy great food from local restaurants, without the wait or the hassle.

About FoodieHub

Who we are

FoodieHub is a food delivery platform that partners with local restaurants, home kitchens and cloud kitchens to bring a wide variety of meals straight to your door. We handle the ordering, the logistics and the delivery so kitchens can focus on cooking and customers can focus on eating.

What began with a handful of neighbourhood restaurants has grown into a network of hundreds of partners, all held to the same standard of quality, freshness and reliability.

Our Mission

Feed people well, fast

Our mission is to make great food accessible to everyone, everywhere, in as little time as possible — without compromising on quality, freshness or the livelihoods of the kitchens we work with.

We measure success not just in orders delivered, but in meals that arrived hot, on time, and exactly as promised.

Our Vision

A kitchen on every corner, in your pocket

We imagine a future where distance is never the reason someone misses out on a great meal — where any kitchen, however small, can reach hungry customers across the city through one simple app.
